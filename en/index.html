About Me

Hi,

I’m a third-year student at Xiamen University, majoring in Photoelectronic Measurement Technology and Instrumentation. Right now I’m in Sapporo as a term-exchange student in Hokkaido University, mainly studying Physics.

My interests lie at the intersection of mechatronics, intelligent instruments, and space exploration. I especially enjoy working on hands-on projects.

Projects

These are the real-world projects I have participated in so far:

  • Soft Robotic Gripper
    Designed and fabricated a soft robotic gripper using Ecoflex material with internal cavity structures. Combined pneumatic control and embedded sensors to enable object grasping and tactile feedback. Neural networks will be used to recognize deformation patterns caused by nearby objects.

    📍 Soft Robotics, Flexible Sensors, Neural Networks

  • Optical Instrument Automation
    Developed a Python library (based on C commands) to control various brands of optical instruments. Achieved one-click experimental platform control, significantly reducing operation cost and human error.

    📍 Instrument Control, Automation, Python

  • Digit Recognition System
    Built a digit recognition system for fixed-wing model aircraft using deep learning. This project integrated image recognition, embedded systems, and flight control mechanisms.

    📍 YOLOv8, Convolutional Neural Networks
